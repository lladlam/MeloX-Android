### 音乐库与下载

- 在线音乐排在本地音乐之前。

- 新增弥乐「我喜欢」分类、综合歌单与批量下载。
- 网易云收藏专辑接入音乐库。

### 播放器与页面

- 播放器标题/更多菜单进入歌手、专辑、百科前先收回播放器。

- 修复预测式返回手势中页面消失后闪回全屏的问题。
- 新增音量条显示开关。
- 专辑详情显示发行日期。

### 歌词与识别

- B站标题候选拆解更稳健，支持斜杠、feat、装饰字符清理。
- 所有歌词源失败时，从音频文件随机截取两个 3 秒片段进行听歌识曲；失败后自动用 9 秒重试。
- 识别 ID 按播放资源持久化，重启后不需重新识别。
- 新增录屏授权后的系统音频识别（MediaProjection），不保存录屏内容。
- 新增听歌识曲桌面快捷方式。

### 其他

- 修复标签栏排序按钮错位、默认启动页不生效、私信页空值崩溃。
- 日文翻译精简并参考 Gramophone 术语，改善窄屏截断。
- Release 构建启用 R8、资源压缩与签名优化。

## MeloX Android 0.7.5 — 2026-11-17

- Version: `0.7.5`, `versionCode 30`; release tag: `0.7.5`.
- Assets: `MeloX-Android-0.7.5-arm64-v8a.apk`, `MeloX-Android-0.7.5-armeabi-v7a.apk`.
- Signing certificate: CN=MeloX (SHA-256 `dfcca9865b87a402d341985a48eb132bd8679dfa6d9d502f365dd16210a5ebe9`).

### Apple Music appearance

- Migrated Home, Library, Explore, Artist and library secondary pages to the Apple Music / Sonify UI.
- Preserved the liquid-glass artist back button, hero palette, reflection, progressive blur and page transitions.
- Explore is now an editorial feed: full-width cards per category with a See More grid behind the chevron.
- Added a first-run appearance picker for MeloX or Apple Music, switchable later in Settings.
- Dark page backgrounds are now pure black.

### Library and downloads

- Online music now appears before Local Music.
- Local Music only shows downloaded songs, albums, artists and downloaded playlists.
- Added a download-progress page with Apple-style thin progress bars for active/paused tasks.
- Added the MeloX Favorites section, unified playlist and batch download.
- NetEase saved albums are now loaded into the library.

### Player and pages

- Player title/more actions collapse the player before opening artist, album and wiki pages.
- Artist, album, playlist and wiki pages use settings-style enter/exit transitions.
- The dock and mini player stay visible while these pages are open.
- Fixed predictive-back flashing the page back to fullscreen after it had been dragged away.
- Added a show/hide volume-slider setting.
- Album details now show the release date.

### Lyrics and recognition

- More robust Bilibili title candidates with slash, feat and decorative-character cleanup.
- When every lyric source fails, two random 3-second audio-file segments are fingerprinted, with a 9-second retry.
- Recognized IDs are persisted per playback resource, so app restarts do not repeat recognition.
- Added optional MediaProjection system-audio recognition after screen-capture consent; no recording is saved.
- Added a song-recognition launcher shortcut.

### Other

- Fixed tab-order button alignment, the default launch-page setting and a Messages null crash.
- Shortened Japanese copy and aligned terminology with Gramophone to reduce narrow-screen truncation.
- Release build uses R8, resource shrinking and signing optimization.
